About Us

🧭

Who Are We?

AstraPen is a student-built platform created to guide beginners and cybersecurity learners toward the right path. Run entirely by one person — a 3rd-year cybersecurity student who went through the same confusion, struggle, and lack of direction that many students face today.

⚡

Why Are We?

AstraPen was created because of the struggle to find a clear direction in cybersecurity. For almost two years, not knowing what to focus on, which skills mattered, or which career path fit. AstraPen exists to give students something better: real guidance, early direction, and the right resources from day one.

🎯

What Is Our Goal?

Our goal is simple: help students discover the ideal cybersecurity path early, and give them everything they need to start specializing with confidence. AstraPen aims to guide learners toward the right specialization, provide structured collections of the best courses, connect learners with valuable certificates and tools, make cybersecurity education accessible to everyone, and expand beyond cybersecurity into computer engineering and more technical fields in the future.
